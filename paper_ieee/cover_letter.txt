We submit the above paper for consideration at the 2027 Eighth IEEE
International Conference on Research in Computational Intelligence and
Communication Networks (ICRCICN 2027).

The paper studies distribution-free uncertainty bands for neural option
pricers. We train physics-informed neural networks for European and American
puts with no labelled prices and wrap them in split, residual-normalised and
Mondrian conformal prediction. Over ten training seeds, split conformal
reaches its nominal coverage for every model. We diagnose and fix an upward
bias in the American network. Mondrian buckets give the most even coverage
and the narrowest bands. The raw PDE residual is a poor difficulty score for
the American put; the complementarity residual of the free-boundary problem
is better, but it does not beat plain split on width. We also compare ten labelled regressors and report static-arbitrage
checks of prices and band edges. All labels are synthetic, and the paper
states this and its other limits plainly.

The work fits the computational intelligence scope of the conference. The
manuscript is original, has not been published, and is not under review
elsewhere. All authors have approved the submission. The paper follows the
IEEE two-column format and is within the 6-page limit. Use of an AI
assistant is disclosed in the Acknowledgment, as the conference requires.
