# venue_research.md

Research date: 10 Oct 2026 (IST). Scope: IEEE or Springer, Scopus-indexed (or with Scopus evidence for earlier editions), computational intelligence / computational finance / intelligent computing, full-paper deadline late Oct 2026 to Jan 2027, attendable by Indian UG students.
Raw pages and Crossref output are saved in `paper_ieee/_verify/`.

## How to read the "Scopus evidence" column
- **Scopus evidence (strong)**: the proceedings title appears in SCImago (scimagojr.com), which is built only from Scopus data. This proves the named earlier edition was indexed in Scopus.
- **IEEE Xplore evidence (medium)**: Crossref lists papers from earlier editions with IEEE DOIs (publisher = IEEE). That proves the earlier proceedings are in IEEE Xplore. IEEE sends Xplore conference content to Scopus, but IEEE itself says "most", not all, publications are covered, so this is strong circumstantial evidence, not proof. IEEE Xplore pages themselves returned "Error 418, unusual traffic" to this worker, so Crossref was used instead.
- **Claim only**: the organiser's own website says "Scopus indexed". Not counted as proof.
- No edition of any conference is guaranteed: every 2027 CFP says papers "will be submitted for possible inclusion in IEEE Xplore, subject to quality requirements". Scopus indexing of a 2027 volume can only be checked after it is published.

## Why ICRCICN 2027 is primary
1. Scope fit: computational intelligence is the headline topic; a PINN + conformal UQ paper is on topic, unlike a sustainability-themed track.
2. Strongest indexing record of the feasible options: earlier editions in IEEE Xplore (2015-2018, 2020, 2025) and SCImago (Scopus) entries for 2015 and 2016; IEEE conference record #73691 is published for 2027; technically co-sponsored by IEEE Kolkata Section and IEEE CIS Kolkata Chapter.
3. Hybrid mode, which helps three students in Mumbai; a surrogate presenter is also allowed.
4. It states the same 10% similarity limit the NMIMS rubric uses, and has an explicit IEEE AI-disclosure rule we can satisfy.
5. Deadline 30 Nov 2026 leaves time for P1-P3 results; the team can still submit earlier (in October) to get the submission receipt for the 15-mark criterion, if the CMT site is already accepting papers.
Risks: the organiser's financial sponsor is a private foundation (Acuminous Research Foundation); the exact CMT link and PDF eXpress / copyright steps were not visible to this worker. Fee is the highest of the Indian options (Rs 8,500 non-member student early).

## Why Comp-SIF 2027 is backup
1. Real IEEE track record: 2025 and 2026 proceedings are in IEEE Xplore; IEEE Bangalore Section is a technical co-sponsor.
2. Cheapest (Rs 5,500 non-member student early) and a firm, earlier deadline (14 Nov 2026 AoE).
3. Weaknesses: off-theme (sustainability) so desk-rejection risk is higher; in person only in Bengaluru; double-blind with arXiv desk-rejection, so the team must NOT post a preprint and must make an anonymous PDF.
Use it if the team wants a decision earlier (1 Jan 2027) or if ICRCICN's CMT/record turns out not to be live. Do not submit the same paper to both at once (dual submission is not allowed by IEEE); pick one by about 7 Nov 2026.
